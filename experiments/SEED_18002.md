# Lambda 1e-7 with training seed 18002

Test one fresh training seed, 18002, replacing 18001. Use the exact saved
target and poison. Train one defended model at fixed lambda 1e-7 and one
undefended model with the same new seed. The old seed-18001 baseline is
checked as provenance only; it is not copied into the new comparison.

All scientific settings remain unchanged: full CIFAR-10, ResNet18, 40 epochs,
batch 128, learning rate 0.1, Nesterov momentum 0.9, weight decay 0.0005,
the existing schedule and augmentation, FP32/TF32, three inner ascent steps,
radius 16/255, and full-network per-example gradients on eight uniformly
sampled penalty images. There is no clipping, warmup, adaptive weighting,
layer restriction, poison generation or additional seed search.

Seed 18002 controls model initialization and dedicated generators for batch
order, augmentation and defense sampling. The two new models share common
initialization, batch order and spatial augmentation draws. The defended run
is attempted first, followed by its undefended control even if the defense
diverges. Numerical failures are recorded without changing settings.
